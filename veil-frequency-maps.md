# Archive of Echoes — Veil Frequency Maps
## Ambient Drone Oscillator Voices
### Reference: A = 432 Hz | Just Intonation | Harmonic Series

Each veil has 16 suggested frequencies for your operator stack.
Layer as many or as few as feels right. Lower voices form the
foundation, upper voices add shimmer and harmonic colour.
Adjust octaves freely — these are seed tones.

## Rendering Notes

- **Length:** 4-6 minutes per loop, seamlessly loopable
- **Dynamics:** Very slow evolution — fade voices in and out over 30-60 second arcs
- **Processing:** Long reverb tails, gentle low-pass filtering on upper voices
- **Stereo:** Wide placement — let different voices occupy different spatial positions
- **Level:** These sit *beneath* conversation — mix quiet enough that they're felt more than heard
- **Crossfade:** When deploying in the app, use 3-5 second crossfades between veils
- **Format:** Export as .ogg (smaller) and .mp3 (fallback) — 128-192kbps is sufficient for ambient texture
